### About me

I'm a developer from Oyo State, Nigeria. I started out building websites more than five years ago, and since 2025 I've been building with React, TypeScript and React Native. I enjoy the details that make an app feel solid: smooth onboarding, login that just works (OTP, Google Sign-In, biometrics), and interfaces that look like the Figma file.
